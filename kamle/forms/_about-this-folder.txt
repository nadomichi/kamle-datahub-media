Blank application forms as PDF (PRC, ST, income, ID card...). Used by SERVICES > link and by the forms portal.

Rules: JPG or PNG photos under 1 MB, PDF under 2 MB. File names in lower case with hyphens, no spaces (e.g. raga-circuit-house.jpg).
Never rename or delete a file the sheet uses. To replace a file, upload the new one with the SAME name.
Everything here is PUBLIC: only blank forms, photos and brochures meant for citizens. Never filled forms or personal documents.
